# Domain Glossary

## Teams

### Full-Member Team
The men's senior national team of one of the 12 ICC Full Members: Afghanistan, Australia, Bangladesh, England, India, Ireland, New Zealand, Pakistan, South Africa, Sri Lanka, West Indies and Zimbabwe.
Not an Associate Member's team (e.g. Netherlands, Scotland, Nepal), not a women's, A, under-19 or development side, and not a franchise or domestic team.

### National Team
A team representing a country (or, for West Indies, a group of countries) in international cricket. The app covers only National Teams that are Full-Member Teams; "National Team" alone never widens that scope.

## Matches

### Completed Match
A Match that has a Result. Not a Live Match, even in the minutes after the final ball.

### Fixture
A scheduled International Match whose toss has not yet happened. A Fixture becomes a Live Match at the toss. Not a Completed Match, and not a series (a series is a group of Fixtures and Matches between the same two teams).

### Format
The kind of International Match being played: Test (up to five days, two Innings per side), ODI (one Innings per side, up to 50 overs each) or T20I (one Innings per side, up to 20 overs each). No other format is in scope.

### International Match
A men's Test, ODI or T20I in which **both** teams are Full-Member Teams. A match between a Full-Member Team and an Associate Member's team is not an International Match in this product, even though the ICC recognises it as international.

### Live Match
An International Match between the toss and its Result. It stays live through every break in play: drinks, lunch, tea, rain or bad-light delays, the interval between Innings, and overnight between the days of a Test. Not a Fixture (toss not yet made) and not a Completed Match.

### Match
Short for International Match. Every Match is, at any moment, exactly one of a Fixture, a Live Match or a Completed Match.

### Result
How a Match ended: a win (with its margin in runs or wickets), a tie, a draw (Tests only), no result, or abandoned. A Match without a Result is a Fixture or a Live Match.

## Scoring

### Innings
One team's turn to bat in a Match. An ODI or T20I has at most one Innings per side; a Test has at most two.

### Score
The one-line summary of a Match's current state: for the batting side, runs/wickets and overs bowled (e.g. `IND 245/6 (42.3 ov)`), plus the target, lead or deficit where one applies. Not a Scorecard.

### Scorecard
The full detail of each Innings of a Match: each batter's runs and how they were out, each bowler's figures, extras and the fall of wickets. A Scorecard contains far more than a Score, which only summarises it.

## Data freshness

### Feed Delay
A condition in which the Score Feed is unavailable or has not updated a Live Match for longer than expected. During a Feed Delay the app shows the delay, not a Stale Score presented as live.

### Last Updated
The time at which the Score Feed last provided data for a Match. Every Score shown carries its Last Updated time.

### Score Feed
The licensed source of Fixtures, Scores, Scorecards and Results for International Matches. It is the only source of match data; data from any other site is not a Score Feed.

### Stale Score
A Score whose Last Updated time is old enough that it may no longer reflect the Live Match. A Stale Score is never presented as live.
